# Support Triage — Product Brief

*Larkspur Utilities · Customer Operations*

## Problem

The support desk receives several hundred tickets a week. Many describe the same underlying issue (for example an outage or a portal problem), and support staff waste time handling them one by one. Critical tickets occasionally wait for days without the dispatch team being aware.

## Goals

1. Give support staff a view of tickets grouped by underlying problem.
2. Give every ticket a consistent category and urgency.
3. Make sure the dispatch team hears about stale critical tickets.

## Users

| User | Need |
|---|---|
| Support advisor | Work through grouped tickets instead of individual duplicates |
| Support team lead | See groups and open workload per status |
| Dispatch team | Receive alerts about critical tickets that are not progressing |

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Priority | Set by the customer-facing advisor: `Low`, `Normal`, `High`, `Critical` |
| Status | `Open`, `In Progress`, `Resolved` |
| Customer area | `Residential`, `Business`, `Municipal` |
| Group | A set of tickets that describe the same problem |
| Escalation | A notification to the dispatch team about a critical ticket |

## Out of scope

* Changing tickets in the support desk tool.
* Customer-facing communication.
